## Notes

- Residential inverters (1-10 kW, 1- and 3-phase): Growatt MIN TL-X / MOD TL3-X, Solis S6-GR1P, Sungrow SG-RS / SG-RT, Polycab PSIS / PSIT and Havells Enviro GTi are catalogued from their datasheets. Luminous NXI: no datasheet could be downloaded (the product pages give capacity and efficiency only), so none is catalogued. Where a datasheet states efficiency per group of models rather than per model it is left empty.
- CEC inverters: none imported. The CEC list has no MPPT count, inputs per MPPT or short-circuit current, all required by InverterSpec. 2343 rows examined; 26 are at 380-440 V and still lack those fields.
- Inverter-duty transformers: not catalogued. The only citable %Z are two BHEL purchase specifications (2.7 MVA 33 kV 6.25 %, 5.5 MVA 33 kV 7 %, see idt_impedance_citations.csv). Both give the LV voltage as a range set per inverter (340-410 V, 500-700 V), and TransformerSpec needs a single lv_kv. No standard %Z ladder for 1.6-12.5 MVA was found, and no purchaser specification with one stated LV voltage could be downloaded (SECI tender documents returned HTTP 500). A project that needs an IDT carries an inline TransformerSpec on the node and records in design_basis.assumptions which cited values it borrowed.
- MCCB DC ratings (250/500/750 V) not catalogued: ProtectionSpec has no AC/DC field and no 1500 V DC ladder was found.
- ACB MTZ1 and MTZ3 not catalogued: only their frame ratings were transcribed, no Icu.
- gPV fuses: 1000 V DC 10x38 (Eaton, third-party mirror) and 1500 V DC 10x85 (Mersen HP15M, manufacturer-hosted) ladders are catalogued.
- VCB 36 kV: ABB VD4 offers 630 A at 16 and 20 kA only; 25 kA starts at 1250 A, so there is no 630 A / 25 kA entry. 1600, 2000 and 2500 A are catalogued at 25, 31.5 and 40 kA (2500 A rated at ambient below 40 C); 3150 A (assembled poles) is not.
- LV-side CT/PT and a 36 kV manufacturer RMU: no citable text-extractable source was found, so none are catalogued.
- Isolators: ABB OT E-series (AC, 200-2500 A, 3/4P) and OTDC (DC, 100-250 A, 1000 V S1.0 and 1500 V S2.0, 2/4P). The 1250-2500 A OT frames carry no short-time withstand because the text layer of their table columns is ambiguous.
- AC SPD Type 2: Finder 7P.23/7P.25 for 230/400 V systems and DEHN DG SE CI 440 FM (single pole, for 400/690 V systems). DC SPD 1500 V is the Finder 7P.23.9.500.x015 entry above.
- 33 kV PT and the 36 kV class arrester with discharge counter come from purchaser specifications (WBSEDCL; a buyer's switchgear surge-arrester specification whose issuer the document does not name), not manufacturer datasheets.
- 800 V-class LT breakers: Schneider MasterPacT MTZ2 H10 (1150 V AC, 800-4000 A, 3P, Icu 50 kA) and ABB Tmax T4/T5 V and T6 L (1000-1150 V AC, 100-800 A, 3P/4P, Icu 12 kA) are catalogued. Siemens 3WL / 3VA and Schneider ComPacT NSX/NS at 800 V and above were not found in a text-extractable source.
- 33 kV inverter-duty transformers with an 800 V LV winding: gap. The only public sources found are manufacturer product pages (for example JDS Transformers, '33/0.800 kV, up to 25 MVA') that state no %Z, and the two BHEL purchase specifications, whose LV windings are 340-410 V and 500-700 V. TransformerSpec needs %Z, so none is catalogued; string-inverter blocks at 800 V use the same project-specific inline spec as central inverters (BHEL PS 439-1304 values, reported as an assumption).
- 11 kV CT, PT and arrester: purchaser (APDCL) specifications, not manufacturer datasheets. The CT carries no ratio because the specification leaves the primary current to the project.
